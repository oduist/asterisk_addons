Call enable your Odoo!

A WEB phone specially designed and make and receive calls in Odoo!

30-Day Free Trial: This module is provided as a 30-day free trial. A license purchase is required to continue using it after the trial period expires. Visit https://oduist.com for pricing and licensing details.
For your convenience, you can download our modules in one package here: https://asterisk_plus.com/r/Sxq

Features

  • True one click dialing experience.
  • Fast contact search and dial.
  • Multi tab browser support (answer / hangup / transfer from any tab).
  • Easy contact creation.
  • Ringing volume control and mute.
  • Favorite contacts.
  • Recent Calls.
  • Floating screen position.
